# SOP
Major Incident Response

# Purpose
This SOP defines how the organization responds to major incidents that produce significant service degradation but do not yet meet the threshold of a critical incident.

# Scope
This SOP applies when one or more core services are degraded, a notable subset of users is affected, or recovery requires cross-team coordination.

# Responsibilities
- Incident Manager: coordinates response and tracks recovery progress
- Operations Team: performs mitigation, validates service health, and monitors customer impact
- Platform Team: supports resiliency, infrastructure, and environment stabilization actions
- Engineering Team: investigates root cause and implements corrective changes
- Management: reviews progress and supports prioritization when required

# Procedure
1. Confirm the scope, impact, and initial symptoms of the incident
2. Assign an incident lead and supporting roles for response coordination
3. Activate the relevant runbook and known-issue documentation
4. Contain and mitigate the impact as quickly as possible
5. Keep stakeholders informed with structured updates at defined intervals
6. Record the cause, resolution steps, and follow-up actions in the incident record
7. Verify service recovery and formally close the incident once stability is confirmed

# Escalation Matrix
- Severity Critical: escalate immediately to leadership and cross-functional response teams
- Severity High: escalate to the incident manager and relevant service owners
- Severity Medium: escalate when impact expands, recovery exceeds expected time, or additional teams are required

# Communication Guidelines
All updates should remain clear, factual, and concise. Updates should describe the current status, current impact, actions underway, and the next expected update time.

# Best Practices
- Use incident runbooks to accelerate recovery
- Keep a consistent timeline of actions and decisions
- Avoid speculative statements and focus on validated information
- Preserve operational context for future incident review and training
