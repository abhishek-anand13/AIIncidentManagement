# SOP
Communication Protocol

# Purpose
This SOP establishes a consistent communication framework for incident updates so that responders, stakeholders, and leadership receive accurate and timely information.

# Scope
This SOP should be followed during active incidents, major service degradation events, and any situation requiring coordinated response across teams.

# Responsibilities
- Incident Manager: owns the communication cadence and stakeholder alignment
- Operations Team: provides status updates from execution activities and monitoring data
- Engineering Team: shares technical findings, mitigation progress, and recovery expectations
- Management: receives concise updates on business impact and response priorities
- Support Team: relays customer impact and escalates emerging issues when necessary

# Procedure
1. Open a communication channel for the active incident
2. Provide an initial update that includes status, impact, and current actions
3. Use consistent severity and status terminology in every message
4. Share updates at defined intervals until the incident is resolved
5. Document key facts, blockers, and decisions in the incident record
6. Close with a clear recovery summary and any follow-up actions

# Escalation Matrix
- Severity Critical: escalate to leadership and cross-functional response teams immediately
- Severity High: escalate to service owners and incident leadership when impact grows or recovery slows
- Severity Medium: escalate when support demand rises or technical resolution requires additional expertise

# Communication Guidelines
Communication should be factual, concise, and action-oriented. Each update should explain what is known, what is being done, what remains unknown, and when the next update will be provided.

# Best Practices
- Avoid speculation and unsupported claims
- Keep messages concise and relevant to the audience
- Maintain a single source of truth for incident status
- Ensure that stakeholder communications reflect the current operational reality
